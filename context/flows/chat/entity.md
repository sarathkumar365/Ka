# Chat: the Ka entity and the composer

Part of the [chat flow](index.md).

`Entity.tsx` is the entity from the design prototype (`Design/extracted/app.html`), not a drawing: one element whose position, size and corners change per phase (`entityStyle.ts`, a port of the prototype's `entity()`), with a faint copy trailing it 700 ms behind. On the empty home it is a 152 px blob whose corners shift every 2.4 s beat (9% here, stronger than the design's 4%, by request); focusing the box turns it into the box's own border; while thinking it shrinks into place and hands over to `ThinkingRing.tsx`, the wobbly ring from the design's entity board (radius 9 ±1.5 px, three drifting sine lobes, a bright arc drifting round at 0.45 turns a second; the owner chose it over the prototype's spinning arc); while a tool runs it is a 44 px bar sliding end to end every 900 ms; answering and idle it sits in the box as a 24 px ring, and waiting for a yes it is a brighter 26 px ring with a halo. Up to six lights count cards waiting in Pending (hidden while typing, thinking or searching). The composer's placeholder carries the status ("Thinking", what the running tool does such as "Searching transactions", "Answer yes or no above" while an approval waits, "Reply or ask something else"), and a hidden status line reads it to screen readers; its chrome dims on the empty home and disappears while typing, and new messages open from 0 to full height (loaded history does not). Its optional `lights` prop fixes the Pending lights and skips the Pending request; the signed-out home screen (`/welcome`) uses `lights={0}`.

**Lining up on phones.** The composer, the entity's stage (so the typing border, the docked ring, the thinking ring, the search bar and the waiting halo) and the bottom of the message list are all lifted by one CSS value, `--dock-b`: the larger of the iPhone home-bar inset (`--safe-b`, from `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`) and the open keyboard (`--kb`, see the PWA flow). The lift is the stage's bottom inset, not part of each position, so the keyboard opening starts no glide: the entity moves with the box instead of trailing it. Before this, the composer moved up by the home bar but the entity's offsets did not, so on an iPhone the text and the ring sat 34 px off the box. The thread follows new text only while the reader is within 80 px of the end (`follow.ts`); scrolling up to read stops it, sending or opening a chat starts it again; the keyboard shrinking the thread also keeps its end in view. While typing, the empty-state words fade out, as the box rises into their place with the keyboard. It glides (`behavior: 'smooth'`), except a freshly opened chat, which opens at its end. The thread has no scrollbar and does not bounce the page. On short screens (height 500 px or less, a sideways phone) the empty blob shrinks to the left and the words sit beside it.

A decided approval folds to one line: "Approved." with the summary, or "Left as it was." It does not say Saved, because the write can still fail; that failure arrives as a notice.
